These terms govern use of the L3 DC OTP Mailer and its authentication support for the private L3 DC Template.

Effective: 26 August 2026

1. Authorized operational use

The authentication service is intended only for personnel whose individual work address is on the private approved-staff list for the L3 DC Template — WEST DEPOT. This public site provides information only and does not grant access.

2. Login-code responsibilities

Enter only your own approved work address. A one-time code is confidential. You must not share, forward, publish, reuse, or request a code on behalf of another person. If a code arrives unexpectedly, ignore it and report repeated unexpected requests through the established internal support process.

3. Prohibited activity

You must not attempt to bypass access controls, automate excessive requests, interfere with service availability, probe security controls, impersonate another user, or use the service for any unlawful or unauthorized purpose.

4. Access and availability

Access may be limited, suspended, or revoked to protect operations, respond to misuse, perform maintenance, or comply with security requirements. The service may be unavailable temporarily and is provided only as an operational support tool.

5. External services

Email delivery, hosting, and abuse protection depend on Google and Cloudflare services. Their respective terms and policies also apply to their processing. The Gmail authorization is limited to sending authentication email and does not permit mailbox reading.

6. Changes

These terms may be updated when the authentication design, service providers, or security requirements change. The effective date on this page identifies the current version.

7. Contact

Questions about these terms can be sent to l3dc.login@gmail.com. Do not include passwords, one-time codes, or operational information in email.
